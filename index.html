Standards & data sources

  1. Humidity: U.S. EPA, A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home — keep indoor humidity below 60%, ideally 30–50%.
  2. Temperature comfort: ASHRAE Standard 55 ranges as summarized by NC Division of Public Health (about 68.5–76°F in winter, 73–80°F in summer).
  3. Outside weather: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), updated about every 15 minutes.
  4. Room readings: HomePod built-in sensor, logged by GitHub Actions. Not a calibrated instrument; treat values as trends.
